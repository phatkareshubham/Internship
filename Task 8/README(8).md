# Task 8 - Superstore Dashboard (Power BI)

##  Project Overview
This project presents an **interactive Power BI dashboard** built using the **Sample - Superstore** dataset.  
The dashboard provides key insights into sales performance, profit trends, product categories, and regional performance.

---

##  Dataset
**Source:** Tableau Sample - Superstore (converted to CSV/Excel)  
**Key Fields:**
- Order Date
- Region
- Category & Sub-Category
- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity
- Ship Mode
- State & City

---

##  Objectives
- Analyze **sales** and **profit trends** over time
- Identify **top-performing regions, categories, and products**
- Visualize **profit margins** to detect underperforming areas
- Provide **interactive filtering** for stakeholders

---

##  Dashboard Pages

### **Page 1 – Executive Overview**
- **KPI Cards**: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Avg. Profit Margin
- **Line Chart**: Sales over Month-Year
- **Donut Chart**: Sales by Category
- **Bar Chart**: Sales by Region
- **Column Chart**: Profit Margin by Sub-Category
- **Slicers**: Region, Category, Year
- **Insights Text Box** for quick findings

### **Page 2 – Detailed Analysis**
- **Top 10 Products** by Sales
- **Top 10 Customers** by Sales
- **Map**: Sales by State
- **Table**: Product, Sales, Profit, Profit Margin (with conditional formatting)
- **Stacked Column Chart**: Sales & Profit by Ship Mode

---

##  How to Use
1. Open **Task 8 - Superstore Dashboard.pbix** in Power BI Desktop.
2. Use **slicers** to filter by Region, Category, or Year.
3. Hover over visuals for additional details via tooltips.
4. Explore both dashboard pages for a full analysis.

---

##  Sample Insights
- West region consistently leads in total sales.
- Technology category shows steady growth.
- Some sub-categories (e.g., Tables) have high sales but low profit margins.
